About this project

'Australian Film & TV in Time' lets you browse Australian film and TV culture over the decades. The project uses film and tv data taken from the National Film and Sound Archive (NFSA) and it is purposefully designed so that users can explore, rather than search following the theories of generous interfaces and information flaneur.

About the NFSA
